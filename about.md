## Professional

I am a backend Software Engineer for [Darktrace](https://www.darktrace.com). I work across various components of our Email product, using a mix of languages including Python, Typescript, and Rust.

I have a working knowledge of Linux, networking, and Kubernetes. I like to dig deep on lower level topics: for example, I enjoy a lot of Matt Godbolt's talks on [benchmarking](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EU_nQh8wg5A) high performance systems and the [architecture of modern CPUs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BVVNtG5dgks).

## Hobbies

I enjoy cooking, video games, and playing guitar. I play Ultimate Frisbee and squash, and I am also trying to pick up tennis.

## Background

I have a Master's degree in Physics, where I leaned into QFT and Theoretical Condensed Matter. My Master's [project](https://github.com/oscarsimpson/part-iii-project-report/blob/main/report.pdf) was on [computational](https://github.com/oscarsimpson/part-iii-project) lattice QCD. I have a general interest in emergent phenomena, which includes topics such as statistical mechanics and topological physics. In particular, at one time I considered entering into a PhD studying [Topological Quantum Field Theory](https://www-thphys.physics.ox.ac.uk/people/SteveSimon/topological2021/TopoBook-Sep28-2021.pdf), which could have applications in quantum computing and superconductivity, although I was usually more interested in the science than any real-world applications.
