WIP based on [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/thai-pork-peanut-curry](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/thai-pork-peanut-curry)

Can use pork belly slices, cut into chunks. Render down fat first. Do not season pork first, red curry paste is already salty enough

Needs more lime 1.5 - 2 maybe. Add lime peel (or zest) to sauce while simmering.
